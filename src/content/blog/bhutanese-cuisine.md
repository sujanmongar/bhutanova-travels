In Bhutan, chillies aren't a seasoning — they're a vegetable. Here's what to try.

## Ema datshi
The national dish: green or red chillies stewed in a sauce of local cheese. Served with red rice at almost every meal.

## Kewa and shamu datshi
The same cheesy sauce with potatoes (*kewa*) or mushrooms (*shamu*) — a gentler start for spice-shy travellers.

## Momos
Steamed dumplings filled with cheese, vegetables or meat, dipped in fiery *ezay* chilli sauce.

## Red rice and buckwheat
Bhutan's nutty red rice grows in the Paro valley. In Bumthang, try *puta* (buckwheat noodles) and *khuli* (buckwheat pancakes).

## Suja
Salty butter tea, offered to every guest — a warm welcome on a cold mountain morning.
