Most first-time visitors see Paro, Thimphu and Punakha — and they're wonderful. But Bhutan's quieter corners are where the kingdom really slows down.

## Phobjikha Valley
A wide glacial valley where endangered black-necked cranes arrive each winter. Walk the Gangtey nature trail at dawn when mist hangs over the marshland.

## Haa Valley
Just over the Chele La pass from Paro, Haa opened to tourists only in 2002. Farmhouse stays, alpine meadows and almost no other travellers.

## Bumthang
The spiritual heartland, with some of Bhutan's oldest temples, a local brewery and buckwheat pancakes you won't find anywhere else.

## Trashigang and the east
A long drive rewarded with textile villages, the Merak-Sakteng highlanders and the country's most authentic festivals.
