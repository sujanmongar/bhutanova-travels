Thimphu is small, walkable and full of character. Here's our ideal first day.

- **Morning:** Circle the National Memorial Chorten with local elders spinning prayer wheels, then drive up to Buddha Dordenma for valley views.
- **Late morning:** Meet Bhutan's national animal, the takin, at the Motithang reserve.
- **Lunch:** Ema datshi and red rice in town.
- **Afternoon:** The Folk Heritage Museum and the textile museum, then watch the traffic police direct cars by hand at the main junction.
- **Evening:** The flag-lowering ceremony at Tashichho Dzong at sunset.
