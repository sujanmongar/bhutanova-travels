Every year, dzong courtyards across Bhutan fill with colour as communities gather for their *tshechu* — a festival honouring Guru Rinpoche, who brought Buddhism to the Himalaya.

## The masked dances
The heart of every tshechu is *cham*: sacred masked dances performed by monks and laymen. Each dance tells a story — the triumph of good over evil, or the journey of the soul after death.

## The thongdrel
On the final morning of many festivals, a giant appliqué thangka is unrolled across the dzong wall before sunrise. Simply seeing it is believed to cleanse one's sins.

## When to go
- **Punakha Drubchen & Tshechu** — February/March
- **Paro Tshechu** — March/April
- **Thimphu Tshechu** — September/October
- **Jambay Lhakhang Drup** (Bumthang) — October/November
