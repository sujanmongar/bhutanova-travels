Bhutan follows a "High Value, Low Volume" tourism policy. The Sustainable Development Fee is a daily fee that funds free healthcare and education, conservation and carbon-neutral development.

## How much is it?

| Traveller | SDF per night |
|---|---|
| Adults (international) | USD 100 |
| Children aged 6–12 | USD 50 |
| Children under 6 | Free |
| Indian nationals | INR 1,200 |

The USD 100 rate applies until **31 August 2027**.

## What's included in our prices
Every Bhutanova package already includes the SDF, so the price you see is the price you pay for the land portion of your trip.

> Rates are set by the Royal Government of Bhutan and may change. We'll always quote the current rate.
