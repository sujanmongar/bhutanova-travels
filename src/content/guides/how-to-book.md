Booking a trip to Bhutan is simpler than most people expect. Here's how it works with us.

## 1. Choose a package or ask for a custom trip
Browse our [tour packages](/tours/) or tell us your dates, interests and budget. We reply to every enquiry within 24 hours.

## 2. Confirm your itinerary and quote
We send a day-by-day itinerary with a clear price breakdown: daily Sustainable Development Fee (SDF), hotels, guide, vehicle and meals.

## 3. Pay a deposit
A deposit secures your hotels and flights. The balance is due before arrival. We accept bank transfer and card payments.

## 4. We apply for your visa
With a scan of your passport, a recent photo and your travel insurance, we apply for your visa online through the Department of Immigration. Approval usually arrives within five working days.

## 5. Book flights into Paro
Only Drukair and Bhutan Airlines fly into Paro. We can book seats for you — useful because flights fill up in festival season.

## 6. Arrive and enjoy
Your guide meets you at arrivals with your visa, a *khadar* (white scarf) and a big smile.

> **Tip:** Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) book out early. Enquire at least 3 months ahead for festival dates.
