Bhutan's climate varies with altitude — from subtropical southern plains to alpine peaks above 7,000 m.

## Spring (March–May)
Mild days, rhododendrons in bloom and the Paro Tshechu. One of the best times to visit.

## Summer (June–August)
Monsoon season: green valleys, afternoon rain and some road delays. Fewer visitors and good hotel availability.

## Autumn (September–November)
Clear skies, crisp air and the best mountain views. Peak season for trekking and festivals, including the Thimphu Tshechu.

## Winter (December–February)
Cold nights but sunny, dry days and clear Himalayan views. Black-necked cranes winter in the Phobjikha valley from November to February.

| Place | Altitude | Summer day | Winter day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paro | 2,250 m | ~25 °C | ~9 °C |

| Punakha | 1,250 m | ~30 °C | ~16 °C |
| Bumthang | 2,600 m | ~22 °C | ~5 °C |
