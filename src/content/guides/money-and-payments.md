## Currency
Bhutan's currency is the **Ngultrum (BTN)**, pegged 1:1 to the Indian Rupee. Indian Rupees are widely accepted too.

## Cash and ATMs
- ATMs in Thimphu, Paro and Phuentsholing accept most international Visa and Mastercard cards; they are scarce elsewhere.
- Bring some **US dollars** in clean, recent notes. You can change them at the airport, banks and larger hotels.
- Carry small notes for tips, souvenirs and temple offerings.

## Cards and digital payments
Larger hotels and handicraft shops take cards, sometimes with a surcharge. Small shops and cafés are mostly cash or local mobile payments.

## Tipping
Tipping is not mandatory but is appreciated. As a guide, many travellers give around USD 10–15 per day to their guide and USD 7–10 per day to their driver.

## Paying for your tour
Tour payments are made to your operator by bank transfer or card before arrival, and cover the SDF, visa, hotels, guide, transport and meals.
