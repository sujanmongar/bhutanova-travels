## Who needs a visa?
All international visitors need a visa, except nationals of **India, Bangladesh and the Maldives**, who receive an entry permit on arrival.

- **Indian nationals** can enter with a valid passport or Voter ID card.
- **Everyone else** must have a visa approved *before* travelling. You'll receive a visa clearance letter; the visa is stamped in your passport on arrival.

## How to apply
Apply online through the Department of Immigration's official portal, or let your tour operator apply for you. You'll need:

- A passport valid for at least 6 months from your date of departure
- A recent passport photo
- Travel insurance covering your full stay
- Payment of the visa fee and the Sustainable Development Fee

## Cost
The visa fee is a one-time **USD 40** per person. The SDF is charged separately per night — see our [SDF guide](/travel-guide/sustainable-development-fee/).

## Guides and hotels
International tourists must stay in tourism-certified accommodation and be accompanied by a licensed Bhutanese guide when travelling beyond Thimphu and Paro. All our packages include both.

## Customs
Declare cash over USD 10,000, electronics you're carrying for personal use, and any tobacco products (duty applies). Antiques and religious artefacts cannot be exported without a permit. Drones require prior permission from the civil aviation authority.

> Rules can change. Always confirm current requirements with the Department of Immigration or the Department of Tourism before you travel.
