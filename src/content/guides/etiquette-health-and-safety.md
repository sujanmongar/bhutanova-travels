## Etiquette
- Walk **clockwise** around chortens, prayer wheels and temples.
- Remove hats and shoes where asked; photography is not allowed inside most temples and dzong shrines.
- Don't point your feet at altars or people, and don't touch religious objects.
- Ask before photographing people, especially monks.
- Smoking is banned in public places.

## Health
- No vaccinations are mandatory, but check routine vaccines and hepatitis A/typhoid with your doctor.
- Drink bottled or filtered water.
- Bhutanese food can be very spicy — ask for "less chilli" (*ema chung*).
- Good hospitals are in Thimphu; travel insurance with medical evacuation is required.

## Safety
Bhutan is very safe with little crime. The main risks are winding mountain roads and altitude, both managed by your driver and guide.
