## By air
**Paro International Airport (PBH)** is Bhutan's only international airport. Two airlines fly there:

- **Drukair** (Royal Bhutan Airlines)
- **Bhutan Airlines**

Common connections include Delhi, Kolkata, Bagdogra, Guwahati, Kathmandu, Bangkok, Singapore and Dhaka. The landing into Paro, between mountain ridges, is one of the most scenic in the world — sit on the left side flying in from Kathmandu for Everest views.

## Overland
Travellers can enter from India at **Phuentsholing** (from Jaigaon, West Bengal), **Gelephu** and **Samdrup Jongkhar**. Phuentsholing to Thimphu is about a 5-hour drive.

## Getting around
There are no trains in Bhutan. Tourists travel by private vehicle with a driver and guide. Domestic flights connect Paro with Bumthang and Yongphulla in the east.
