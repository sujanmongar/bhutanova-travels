With some of the highest unclimbed mountains on Earth, Bhutan is a trekking destination like no other. Trails lead through rhododendron forest, yak-herder camps and remote villages to glacial lakes beneath Jomolhari and the Himalayan crest.

All our treks are fully supported: licensed trekking guide, cook, horsemen, tents and meals. We rate every route for difficulty so you can pick the right challenge.
