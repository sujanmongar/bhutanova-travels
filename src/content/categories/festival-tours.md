Tshechus are Bhutan's great religious festivals, held in dzong courtyards across the country to honour Guru Rinpoche. Monks and laymen perform sacred masked dances (*cham*) while whole communities gather in their finest clothes.

The most famous — Paro, Thimphu and Punakha — draw crowds from every valley, and at dawn on the final day of many festivals a giant *thongdrel* (thangka) is unrolled, said to bring liberation to all who see it.

Our festival tours are timed around the lunar calendar, with good viewing spots arranged by your guide and time to explore the surrounding valleys.
